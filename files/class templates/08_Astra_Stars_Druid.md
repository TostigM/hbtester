## Stars Druid Subclass Features

- **L3: Star Map** — Gain Holy Symbol star map. Always have **Guidance** and **Guiding Bolt** prepared. Cast Guiding Bolt a number of times equal to prof bonus without expending slot (recover on long rest).
- **L3: Starry Form** — Spend Wild Shape as bonus action to assume Starry Form (immaterial starlight body). Lasts 10 min or until incapacitated/ends. Three forms:
  - **Archer**: Ranged spell attack (range 60 ft), 1d8 + WIS mod radiant damage, bonus action
  - **Chalice**: When you cast spell that restores HP, you or creature within 30 ft regains 1d8 + WIS mod HP
  - **Dragon**: When making INT/WIS check or concentration save, treat d20 roll as 10 or higher
- **L6: Cosmic Omen** — After long rest, roll d6: odd = Weal, even = Woe. When a creature within 30 ft makes attack roll, save, or ability check, spend reaction to roll d6 and add (Weal) or subtract (Woe) to their roll. Prof bonus uses per long rest.
- **L10: Twinkling Constellations** — Starry Form improvements. Archer damage becomes 2d8. Chalice healing becomes 2d8. Dragon grants 20 ft fly speed (hover). Can change which form at start of each turn.
- **L14: Full of Stars** — While in Starry Form, resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage.

## Behavior AI Priorities

1. Opening: Starry Form (Archer or Chalice) + concentration spell (Spirit Guardians at L5, Faerie Fire low level)
2. Archer form for consistent ranged damage; Chalice if allies taking damage; Dragon for concentration-critical fights
3. Cosmic Omen (L6+): Reaction to adjust ally save or enemy attack
4. Guiding Bolt free casts (PB uses/long rest) on first enemy
5. Potent Spellcasting (L7+): Use cantrips when slots depleted (Produce Flame, Thorn Whip with +WIS damage)
6. Heal with Cure Wounds/Healing Word when allies drop
7. Upcast key spells (Heal, Greater Restoration, Mass Cure Wounds) at mid-high levels
