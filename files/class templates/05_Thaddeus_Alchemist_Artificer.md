## Artificer Class Features (2024 Forge of the Artificer)

- **Spellcasting (INT-based):** Half-caster progression. Prepared spells = INT mod + half artificer level (rounded up).
- **Magical Tinkering:** Imbue tiny nonmagical objects with minor magical properties.
- **Infuse Item** (level 2): Infuse mundane items with magical properties. Known infusions and active infusions scale with level.
- **Right Tool for the Job** (level 2): Magically create artisan's tools with 1 hour of work.
- **Subclass at level 3:** Alchemist
- **Tool Expertise** (level 6): Double proficiency bonus with tools you're proficient with
- **Flash of Genius** (level 7): Reaction to add INT mod to an ally's ability check or save within 30 ft
- **Magic Item Adept** (level 10): Attune up to 4 magic items; craft common/uncommon magic items in half time/cost
- **Spell-Storing Item** (level 11): Store 1st or 2nd-level spell in object for repeated use
- **Magic Item Savant** (level 14): Attune up to 5 magic items; ignore class/race/level requirements on magic items
- **Magic Item Master** (level 18): Attune up to 6 magic items
- **Soul of Artifice** (level 20): +1 saves per attuned magic item; end an attunement to avoid dropping to 0 HP

---

## Alchemist Subclass Features

- **Level 3: Tools of the Trade** — Proficiency with Alchemist's Supplies and Herbalism Kit. Halve potion-crafting time.
- **Level 3: Alchemist Spells** — Always prepared: Purify Food and Drink, Ray of Sickness, Healing Word, Melf's Acid Arrow, Cure Wounds, Flaming Sphere, Mass Healing Word, Death Ward, Raise Dead (progressive)
- **Level 3: Experimental Elixir** — Create elixirs as a bonus action with Alchemist's Supplies. Number = INT mod (min 1). Elixir types: Healing, Swiftness, Resilience, Boldness, Flight, Transformation (random or chosen at higher levels)
- **Level 5: Alchemical Savant** — Add INT mod to one healing or damage roll of a spell cast via Alchemist's Supplies per spell
- **Level 9: Restorative Reagents** — Elixirs grant 2d6 + INT mod temporary HP. Cast Lesser Restoration without slots using Alchemist's Supplies (uses = prof bonus/long rest)
- **Level 15: Chemical Mastery** — Resistance to Acid and Poison damage; immunity to Poisoned condition; cast Greater Restoration and Heal without slots 1/long rest each

## Simulation Notes

**Primary combat pattern:**
- Pre-combat: Distribute Experimental Elixirs to party (Healing, Resilience, Swiftness)
- Opening: Buff with Haste or Faerie Fire
- Middle rounds: Concentration on Web/Haste; attack with crossbow or cast area spells with Alchemical Savant INT boost
- Support: Flash of Genius (reaction) to save critical rolls; Healing Word (bonus action) for emergencies

**Key strengths:** Pre-combat buff economy, Flash of Genius save rescue, long-duration utility spells
**Key weaknesses:** Half-caster spell progression (lags behind full casters); limited combat damage; Experimental Elixir randomness before level 15

**Behavior AI priorities:**
1. Pre-combat: Create and distribute elixirs (bonus action during exploration)
2. Combat opening: Apply Haste to Garrick or Mira (concentration)
3. Support: Flash of Genius reaction if ally would fail critical save
4. Healing: Healing Word (bonus action) or Cure Wounds when ally bloodied
5. Damage: Alchemical Savant boosted spells when positioning allows
