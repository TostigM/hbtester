## Valor Subclass Features

- **L3: Combat Inspiration** — Creature with BI die can use as reaction to add to AC vs an attack OR to add to damage roll. Valor Bard uses martial weapons, medium armor, shields as spellcasting foci.
- **L3: Bonus Proficiencies** — Medium armor, shields, martial weapons
- **L6: Extra Attack** — Attack twice with Attack action. Can replace one attack with a cantrip.
- **L14: Battle Magic** — When taking Attack action, cast a bard spell with a casting time of 1 action as a bonus action (once per turn limit)
