## Signature Tactical Patterns by Tier

### Tier 1 (Levels 1–4)
- **Combat opener:** Hand Crossbow with Sneak Attack (needs ally adjacent to target OR advantage)
- **Cunning Action:** Hide (become unseen for advantage on next attack = reliable Sneak Attack)
- **Defense:** Good DEX save + AC; low HP means avoid being targeted
- **Utility:** Thieves' Tools for locks/traps, Stealth for scouting

### Tier 2 (Levels 5–10)
- **Sneak Attack combo:** Vex mastery on Hand Crossbow — first hit grants advantage on next attack, reliable Sneak Attack chain
- **Uncanny Dodge** for mitigating big hits
- **Evasion** vs. area spells (Fireball, Dragon Breath)
- **Fast Hands** for item manipulation in combat
- **Positioning:** Thief climbing and jumping to unusual angles

### Tier 3 (Levels 11–15)
- **Reliable Talent** makes Mira almost impossible to fail a proficient skill check
- **Sneak Attack** dealing 6d6–8d6 consistently
- **Use Magic Device** lets Mira use scrolls/wands she wouldn't normally access
- **Key tactical option:** Cunning Action Hide every turn for reliable advantage

### Tier 4 (Levels 16–20)
- **Thief's Reflexes** — two turns at start of combat = devastating alpha strike potential
- **Elusive** — enemies lose advantage on attacks against Mira, dramatically increasing survivability
- **Sneak Attack** at 9d6–10d6 with double-attack Thief's Reflexes = burst damage peak
- **Stroke of Luck** as emergency button (turn miss into hit, or succeed on skill)

## Simulation Notes

**Primary combat pattern:**
- **Opening:** Hide (Cunning Action bonus action) then attack with advantage for guaranteed Sneak Attack, OR shoot first while ally is adjacent to target
- **Main rounds:** Two attacks per action (at L5+ via Extra Attack? **NO: Rogue doesn't get Extra Attack.** Mira attacks once per action for Sneak Attack, uses Cunning Action for Hide/Dash/Disengage)
- **Vex mastery:** First hit grants advantage on subsequent attack against same target (Mira's "next attack" comes next turn; Vex timer resets start of next turn)
- **Emergency:** Uncanny Dodge (halve incoming damage), Evasion (negate area spell damage), Lucky point to reroll

**Stealth specialty:** Cloak + Boots of Elvenkind + Expertise Stealth + Reliable Talent means Mira can rarely fail at reconnaissance

**Key weaknesses:** Low CON/HP, single attack per turn (no Extra Attack), limited AoE damage, depends on positioning for Sneak Attack setup.

**Behavior AI priorities:**
1. **Turn 1:** Hide (bonus action) OR attack from starting position if already has advantage
2. **Sneak Attack setup:** Always prioritize getting advantage (hide, flank, ally adjacency)
3. **Target selection:** Focus highest-threat enemy, particularly spellcasters or damage-dealers (to apply pressure)
4. **Positioning:** Stay at ranged distance (80 ft Hand Crossbow), use Thief mobility to get to vantage points
5. **Resource management:** Uncanny Dodge for big hits only (not for weak attacks); Ring of Evasion charges for big area spells
6. **Solo endurance:** Kite aggressively with Cunning Action Dash/Disengage, use Hide to reset advantage each turn
