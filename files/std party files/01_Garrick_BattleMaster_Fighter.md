## Simulation Notes

**Primary combat pattern:** Engages in melee, uses Weapon Mastery (Sap) to impose disadvantage on priority targets, burns Superiority Dice on Trip Attack to prone dangerous foes and grant party advantage, holds Riposte for reaction damage on missed enemy attacks.

**Emergency buttons:** Action Surge (double attacks), Second Wind (self-heal), Indomitable (save reroll).

**Key weaknesses:** Low WIS/INT/CHA save profile (mitigated slightly by Resilient WIS at L4 and Lucky at L14). Limited ranged contribution.

**Behavior AI priorities:**
1. Engage highest-threat melee enemy
2. Use Trip Attack on large enemies or spellcasters with concentration
3. Use Sap mastery on the most dangerous attacker each turn
4. Save Action Surge for boss encounters or focus-fire moments
5. Use Second Wind when below 50% HP
