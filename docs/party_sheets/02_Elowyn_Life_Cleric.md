## Origin Feat (from Human lineage)

**Healer** — Grants a Healer's Kit that stabilizes a creature for no action cost; once per short rest as a Utilize action you can use a Healer's Kit to restore 1d6 + 4 HP + character level. Highly relevant for a healer character.

## Divine Order: Thaumaturge

**Features granted at level 1:**
- Learn one additional Cleric cantrip (**chosen: Sacred Flame** — primary ranged damage cantrip)
- When you make an INT (Arcana or Religion) check, you can use your WIS modifier for the check instead of INT

**Weapon/Armor Proficiencies (Cleric base):** Light armor, Medium armor, Shields, Simple weapons
- *Note: Thaumaturge does NOT add heavy armor or martial weapons (that's the Protector order). This creates a more caster-focused cleric with lower AC than Protector variant.*

**Primary weapon:** Warhammer (simple weapon) with Shield
- *Warhammer is Cleric proficient (simple weapon) and is fine for backup melee*
- *Primary combat contribution is spells, not weapon attacks*

## Simulation Notes

**Primary combat pattern:** Opens with Bless or Spirit Guardians (Tier 2+), maintains concentration aggressively (War Caster at L12), bonus-action attacks with Spiritual Weapon, uses Sacred Flame / Toll the Dead as default cantrip damage when not casting leveled spells, reserves slots for healing emergencies.

**Healing philosophy:** Prefer Healing Word (bonus action, low slot cost) to revive downed allies over Cure Wounds. Use Mass Healing Word when 3+ allies injured. Save higher-level slots for Revivify / Raise Dead emergencies.

**Key weaknesses:** Lower AC than Protector cleric (no heavy armor), low DEX saves, depends on concentration for biggest damage output (Spirit Guardians).

**Behavior AI priorities:**
1. If any ally at 0 HP: Healing Word to revive (before other spellcasting if urgent)
2. If encounter is hard/deadly: open with Spirit Guardians
3. If encounter is easy/medium: open with Bless, then Spirit Guardians next turn
4. Bonus action always: Spiritual Weapon (L3+) or Healing Word as needed
5. Maintain concentration on highest-value concentration spell
6. Use Preserve Life channel divinity when multiple allies wounded
7. Save Divine Intervention for critical boss turns (L10+)
