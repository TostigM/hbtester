## Draconic Sorcery Subclass Features

- **L3: Draconic Resilience** — HP max +3 and +1 per sorc level. AC = 13 + DEX while unarmored.
- **L3: Dragon Ancestor** — Choose dragon type (e.g., **Red** for fire). Speak Draconic. Advantage on Charisma checks vs dragons.
- **L3: Elemental Affinity (Spell)** — When casting spell that deals damage of your ancestor type, add CHA mod to one damage roll.
- **L6: Elemental Affinity (Resistance)** — Spend 1 SP as reaction when taking your ancestor's damage type, gain resistance to that damage for 1 minute.
- **L14: Dragon Wings** — Bonus action, sprout wings for 1 hour (or end with bonus action). Fly speed equal to walk speed.
- **L18: Draconic Presence** — Action: spend 5 SP, emanate 60 ft aura for 1 min. Each creature starting turn in aura or entering it must make WIS save or frightened or charmed (your choice). Concentration.

## Metamagic Summary (at L20)

4. **Empowered Spell** (1 SP): Reroll up to CHA mod damage dice, use new totals

## Behavior AI Priorities

1. Innate Sorcery active: advantage on Sorcerer spell attacks + DC bonus (2 uses/long rest; save for tough fights)
2. Opening vs group: Fireball with Empowered Metamagic (reroll damage dice); Elemental Affinity adds CHA mod damage
3. Opening vs single: Twinned Chromatic Orb (2 targets) or Scorching Ray
4. Boss fight: Heightened Spell on Polymorph/Hold Monster/Disintegrate to push through save
5. Quickened Fireball: cast as bonus action, attack with cantrip for action (Mind Sliver gives save disadvantage for party's next spell)
6. Counterspell reaction against dangerous enemy spells
7. Shield reaction against big hits
8. Dragon Wings (L14+): fly to flanking position or retreat
9. Draconic Presence (L18+): boss fights with multiple enemies (5 SP for charm/fear aura)
10. L20 Arcane Apotheosis: free Metamagic every turn during Innate Sorcery
