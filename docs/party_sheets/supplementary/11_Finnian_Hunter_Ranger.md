## Hunter Subclass Features (2024)

- **L3: Hunter's Lore** — When you mark target with Hunter's Mark, learn its Immunities, Resistances, Vulnerabilities (if any)
- **L3: Hunter's Prey** — Choose each long rest:
  - **Colossus Slayer**: Once per turn, deal extra 1d8 damage to creature below its HP maximum
  - **Horde Breaker**: Once per turn, make one additional attack with same weapon against different creature within 5 ft of original target (not previously attacked this turn)
- **L7: Defensive Tactics** — Choose each long rest:
  - **Escape the Horde**: Opportunity attacks against you have disadvantage
  - **Multiattack Defense**: When hit by attack, attacker has disadvantage on subsequent attacks against you this turn
- **L11: Superior Hunter's Defense** — When damaged, reaction to halve the damage. WIS-mod uses/long rest.
- **L15: Superior Hunter's Prey** — When you damage a creature with Hunter's Mark, you can deal same Hunter's Mark damage (1d6 or 1d10 at L20) to one other creature you choose within 30 ft of target.

## Behavior AI Priorities

1. Turn 1: Hunter's Mark on priority target (free cast via Favored Enemy)
2. Attack action: 2× Longbow (Extra Attack at L5+), Archery FS gives +2 to hit, Sharpshooter allows -5/+10 on tough targets
3. Colossus Slayer triggers on enemies below full HP (consistent extra 1d8)
4. Slow weapon mastery on Longbow: -10 ft speed to target on hit (kite melee threats)
5. Vex mastery on Shortsword (melee): advantage on next attack if first hits (fallback for close combat)
6. Concentration: Hunter's Mark primary; Conjure Animals at L9+ for action economy
7. Spike Growth (L5+): area denial against multiple melee enemies
8. Superior Hunter's Defense (L11+): reaction to halve damage when hit hard
9. Nature's Veil (L14+): bonus action invisibility to reposition
10. Precise Hunter (L17+): advantage vs marked target — always hit on reasonable rolls
