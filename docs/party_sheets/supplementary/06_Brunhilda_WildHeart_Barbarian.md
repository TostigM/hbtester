## Wild Heart Subclass Features

- **L3: Animal Speaker** — Cast Beast Sense and Speak with Animals as rituals. Wisdom is spellcasting ability.
- **L3: Rage of the Wilds** — Choose each rage: **Bear** (resistance to all damage except Force/Necrotic/Psychic/Radiant), **Eagle** (Disengage + Dash as part of rage bonus action; repeat as bonus action), **Wolf** (allies adv on attacks vs enemies within 5 ft of you)
- **L6: Aspect of the Wilds** — Long-rest swap: gain **darkvision 60 ft**, or **climb speed = walk speed**, or **swim speed = walk speed**
- **L10: Nature Speaker** — Cast Commune with Nature as ritual (changes with terrain)
- **L14: Power of the Wilds** — Choose each rage: **Owl** (fly speed = walk speed while raging), **Panther** (adv on DEX saves; can Dash as bonus action without using rage-dash), **Salmon** (adv on STR checks and saves)

## Behavior AI Priorities

1. First turn: Rage as bonus action (choose Bear for tough fights, Wolf if allies in melee, Eagle for skirmishing)
2. Reckless Attack every turn by default; switch to Brutal Strike at L9+ against high-AC foes
3. Cleave mastery on Greataxe: sweep adjacent enemies
4. Stay engaged; rage resistance rewards being hit
5. Persistent Rage (L15+): don't worry about losing rage from inactivity rounds
