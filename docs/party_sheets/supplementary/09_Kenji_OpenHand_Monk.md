## Open Hand Subclass Features

- **L3: Open Hand Technique** — When you hit with Flurry of Blows attack, choose one:
  - **Addle**: Target can't make opportunity attacks until end of your next turn
  - **Push**: Target makes STR save or pushed up to 15 ft away (no size restriction)
  - **Topple**: Target makes DEX save or prone
- **L6: Wholeness of Body** — Bonus action, regain Martial Arts die + WIS mod HP. Number of uses = prof bonus per long rest.
- **L11: Fleet Step** — When you take a bonus action that isn't Step of the Wind, you can take Step of the Wind as part of the same bonus action.
- **L17: Quivering Palm** — Spend 4 FP when you hit a creature with unarmed strike. Creature can't remove vibration. Action to end vibrations: creature makes CON save or takes 10d12 force damage (half on success). Vibrations end when you die, willingly end them, or don't attack the creature on your subsequent turns.

## Behavior AI Priorities

1. Attack action + Martial Arts bonus strike by default
2. Flurry of Blows (1 FP) when engaging groups or needing CC; apply Open Hand Technique (Topple vs single priority, Push to reposition, Addle for grouped melee)
3. Stunning Strike (1 FP) against high-value targets (bosses, spellcasters)
4. Patient Defense when low HP and tank is engaged (Dodge + Disengage)
5. Step of the Wind to reposition (free base Dash; 1 FP for Dash+Disengage)
6. Wholeness of Body (L6+) when below 50% HP, bonus action
7. Quivering Palm (L17) on boss: set up and trigger on follow-up turn
8. Superior Defense (L18) vs multi-hit scenarios (3 FP, resistance to all non-Force for 1 min)
