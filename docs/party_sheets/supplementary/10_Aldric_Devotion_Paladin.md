## Devotion Subclass Features

- **L3: Sacred Weapon** — Channel Divinity: Bonus action imbue weapon. +CHA mod to attack rolls; weapon sheds bright light 20 ft/dim 20 ft; weapon deals radiant damage (if has damage dice); lasts 10 min or until dropped. Channel Divinity uses regain on short/long rest.
- **L3: Oath Spells** — Always prepared: Protection from Evil and Good, Shield of Faith, Aid, Zone of Truth, Beacon of Hope, Dispel Magic, Freedom of Movement, Guardian of Faith, Commune, Flame Strike (added progressively)
- **L7: Aura of Devotion** — You and creatures within 10 ft can't be Charmed while you're conscious.
- **L15: Smite of Protection** — Aura of Protection grants resistance to all damage when you Smite (Divine Smite or Paladin's Smite) for 1 min.
- **L20: Holy Nimbus** — Bonus action: emanate bright light 30 ft for 10 min. Enemies at start of their turn in light take 10 radiant damage. Advantage on saves vs spells by fiends and undead. Once per long rest.

## Behavior AI Priorities

1. Opening: Engage melee threat; activate Sacred Weapon (Channel Divinity) on boss fights
2. Cast Bless if no concentration already; or Shield of Faith on self/ally
3. Divine Smite on crits (2× damage dice) or on first strike against boss
4. Lay on Hands (pool of 5× level) as bonus action to heal ally to stabilize from 0 HP
5. Maintain Aura of Protection positioning; stay within 10 ft of squishy allies (30 ft at L18+)
6. Heroism (if prepared) for concentration buff granting temp HP each turn
7. Guardian of Faith (L13+) as pre-boss setup
8. Smite of Protection (L15+): Divine Smite → resistance to all damage for 1 minute. Activate on tough fights.
9. Holy Nimbus (L20): bonus action on boss fights for bright light + radiant damage aura.
