## Origin Feat (from Human lineage)

**Magic Initiate (Wizard)** — Learn 2 wizard cantrips + 1 1st-level wizard spell (1/long rest free).
- **Cantrips chosen:** Minor Illusion, Message (utility flexibility; damage cantrips already come from class)
- **Free 1st-level spell:** Find Familiar (1/long rest) — lets Varian maintain a familiar even after losing it in combat without needing to expend a slot

*Note: Cantrips from Magic Initiate count as wizard spells, and Varian can change them on level up per 2024 rules.*

## Wizard Class Features (baseline)

- **Spellcasting:** INT-based, spellbook caster (prepare from spellbook each day equal to level + INT mod)
- **Arcane Recovery:** Once per day on short rest, recover spell slots totaling half wizard level (rounded up)
- **Ritual Adept:** Can cast any spell with Ritual tag as ritual (no slot needed)
- **Wizard subclass unlocked at level 3:** Evocation (Evoker)

**Primary weapon:** Quarterstaff (simple; used with spellcasting focus / wand)
- Not a primary combat weapon; Varian attacks with cantrips

---

## Spellbook Growth

Wizard begins with 6 spells in spellbook at level 1, gains 2 new spells per wizard level (from level-ups), plus any spells copied from scrolls/other spellbooks (1 GP cost irrelevant for sim).

## Simulation Notes

**Primary combat pattern:**
- **Opening:** Assess threats, cast single highest-value AoE or control spell (Fireball with Sculpt Spells excluding allies, Web for lockdown, Hypnotic Pattern for mass save-or-incapacitate)
- **Middle rounds:** Maintain concentration, use cantrips for damage (Fire Bolt), hold Shield/Counterspell as reactions
- **Emergency:** Misty Step to escape, Shield to block fatal hit, Counterspell to stop enemy spell

**Concentration management:** War Caster + Resilient (CON) gives advantage on concentration saves + proficiency bonus. Varian is the party's most reliable concentration maintainer.

**Key weaknesses:** Low HP (d6 hit die + Tough not taken), no armor training (depends on Mage Armor then Robe of the Archmagi), squishy in melee, concentration stacking fails if hit hard enough.

**Behavior AI priorities:**
1. **If enemies concentrated AND large number:** Open with Fireball (Sculpt Spells to exclude allies)
2. **If single strong enemy:** Open with save-or-suck (Hypnotic Pattern, Hold Monster, Polymorph, Maze at L15+)
3. **If enemy casts dangerous spell:** Counterspell (reaction, almost always unless slot-critical)
4. **If hit by attack that would reduce below 50% HP:** Shield (reaction)
5. **If threatened in melee:** Misty Step (bonus action)
6. **Concentration priority:** Always maintain highest-value concentration spell; do not break for flavor
7. **Slot management:** Arcane Recovery during short rest to refresh mid-day
